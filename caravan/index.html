What's inside

A cozy little story

Tiny Caravan rides with you on real trips. You are the passenger, your little food caravan does the work. Every town, river and traffic jam you pass becomes play.

🗺️Real trips, real placesNew towns give coins, new districts and states give stamps.
🎣Fish in real riversCrossing a river? Cast your net and catch local fish.
🍲Jam potStuck in traffic? The pot cooks. Longer jam, bigger meal.
🚐Cart to camperGrow your food caravan and take a cat, dog or bird along.
