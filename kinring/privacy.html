Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Kinring ("the app") is made by Md Alauddin (Alauddin Studio). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: your family list never leaves your phone, there is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Your family list

The people you add (name, relation, optional age, how often you want to make time) and the times you mark as done are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. The app does not read your phone's contacts; you type the names yourself.

Sharing

If you tap "Share", the app makes a picture on your phone and opens your phone's share menu. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether Premium is active using an anonymous ID. We never see your card details.

Notifications

The daily reminder is planned on your phone, at the time you pick. No push server is involved. You can turn it off in Settings.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No ads and no advertising ID
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Children

The app is made for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can delete all app data at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app. For questions or requests, including GDPR and CCPA requests, email hello@alauddinstudio.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
