Help

Questions and answers

What is free and what is Premium?

Free: read all 114 surahs with the English meaning, listen, and Ramadan times. Premium: all memorization and revision tools, with a 7-day free trial.

Where does the Quran text come from?

The Uthmani text is from Tanzil.net, used exactly as published. The recitation is by Aaqib Azeez, released to the public domain on Wikimedia Commons.

Why is there no audio for some surahs?

We add recitation surah by surah after checking every ayah. Until then you can read and memorize those surahs.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Zaytun → Cancel Subscription.
Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Zaytun → Cancel.
Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

I bought Premium but the app still asks me to pay.

Open Settings and tap Restore, using the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If that does not help, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or the Google Play Help Center.

How do I delete my data?

Zaytun → Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. Your progress is only on your phone; we have no account for you.
