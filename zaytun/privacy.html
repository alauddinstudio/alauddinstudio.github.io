Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Zaytun ("the app") is made by Md Alauddin (Alauddin Studio). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: your progress never leaves your phone, there is no account, no ads and no tracking across other apps.

Your progress

The surah you are memorizing, the ayat you have memorized, your revision dates, your streak and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them, under your control.

Microphone

If you tap "Record", the app asks for the microphone and records your recitation into a file on your phone so you can listen to it. The recording is never uploaded and is replaced the next time you record.

Ramadan times

You pick your city from a list. Suhoor and iftar times are calculated on your phone. The app does not ask for your location.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether Premium is active using an anonymous ID. We never see your card details.

Notifications

The daily reminder and the Friday report are planned on your phone. No push server is involved. You can turn them off in Settings.

Crash reports and app usage

To find bugs and improve the app, we use Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics and Remote Config). Google processes this data for us as a service provider.

  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, it sends technical details: the error, the app version, the phone model and system version.
  • App usage: simple events, such as which screens are opened, whether the Premium page was shown and whether a purchase was made.
  • These come with a random app ID made by Firebase and an approximate region (country or city) that Google works out from your IP address. They are not linked to your name, email or advertising ID.
  • The app does not collect the advertising ID and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your progress, recordings and settings are never sent.
  • Remote Config lets us change simple settings, such as the text on the Premium page, without a new app version.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No ads and no advertising ID
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Children

The app is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can delete all app data at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app. For questions or requests, including GDPR and CCPA requests, email hello@alauddinstudio.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
