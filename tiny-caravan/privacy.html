Privacy Policy – Tiny Caravan

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Tiny Caravan: Road Trip Game (the "Game") handles information. The Game is developed by Alauddin Studio ("we", "us"). By using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Your location (the heart of the Game)

The Game turns your real trip into play, so it uses your device's location only while a ride you started is running.

  • When: only after you tap Start ride and confirm you are a passenger. It stops when the ride ends. The Game never asks for "Always" location access.
  • In the background: if you put the phone away during a ride, the ride keeps counting. On iPhone you see the blue location indicator; on Android you see a notification ("Tiny Caravan is on the road").
  • What it is used for: counting kilometers, finding the towns, districts, regions and rivers you pass (from map data stored inside the Game), and drawing your route on the trip card, trip video and map poster.
  • Where it goes: your route and places stay on your phone. We do not send them to our servers (we have none) and do not share or sell them. The Game works offline.
  • Searching for a place: when you type a place name in "Where to?", the words you type are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search service (run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation) to find matching places. Only the typed words are sent, plus the technical data any internet request has (such as your IP address). Your location and route are not sent. If you do not type a place, nothing is sent. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Sharing: a trip video or map picture leaves your phone only if you tap Share and choose where to send it.
  • Deleting: uninstalling the Game deletes all saved trips.

2. Other information

You play without any account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address.

  • Game progress (coins, km, stamps, album, caravan, settings, test notes) is saved on your own device and never sent to us.
  • Battery level is read during a ride for the optional test log on your phone. It is not sent anywhere.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional reward videos provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location (from the IP address, not from your ride)
  • Device type, operating system and app usage information related to ads

The Game does not give your ride location to Google or any ad network. This information is collected directly by Google, not by us. Please read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent for personalized ads before they are shown. You can change your choice at any time.

4. In-app purchases and subscription

Purchases and the Travel Pass subscription are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can deliver the item. You can cancel the subscription at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

5. Safety

The Game is for passengers. Never play while driving. The Game asks at the start of every ride whether you are a passenger.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your choices

  • Location: you can deny or turn off location for the Game in your device settings at any time. You can still play the Demo ride and use the home base, shop and album.
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to allow or stop tracking.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • You can delete all Game data on your phone by uninstalling the Game.

8. Data security

We do not run our own servers. Your trips and progress stay on your device; the only thing we send out is the place name you type into the destination search. Third-party services (Google, Apple) protect data according to their own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add an optional cloud save. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
