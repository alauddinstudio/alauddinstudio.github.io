Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap Start ride when you are a passenger in a car, bus or train. Your caravan rides with you: new areas, towns, rivers and traffic jams give you coins, markets, fish and bingo. Put the phone away, and open it now and then. At the end you get a trip card and a short trip video. Want to see it first? Tap Try a demo ride on the home screen.

Can I play while driving?

No. Tiny Caravan is for passengers only. If you tap "I am driving", no ride starts.

Why does the game need my location?

Your real trip moves the caravan. The game uses location only during a ride you start, to count km and find the places you pass. Your route stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Maps, towns, rivers and the whole game are inside the app. Only reward videos and purchases need internet.

The km stopped counting.

Check that location is allowed for Tiny Caravan ("While Using the App" is enough). On Android, allow the notification so the ride keeps going with the screen off, and turn off battery saving for the game. In tunnels and the metro there is no GPS; km are added when the signal returns.

Does it use a lot of battery?

GPS uses some battery on long trips. For rides over 3 hours we suggest a charger or power bank.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, so the game works offline. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase and your receipt.

How do I cancel the Travel Pass?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tiny Caravan → Cancel. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tiny Caravan → Cancel. The pass keeps working until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

App Store: visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: see Google Play refunds.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in Europe and the UK are asked for their ad choices when the game starts.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account: everything is on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes all trips and progress.
