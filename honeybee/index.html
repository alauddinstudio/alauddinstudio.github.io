A cozy little story

Grandpa Bramble, the old beekeeper, hands you his hives. Walk little Honey the bear cub around the farm, collect honeycomb from the bees and turn it into honey jars, candles and pretty gift boxes.

🏡Hut to manorFive levels, from a Flower Meadow to the golden Honey Manor.
