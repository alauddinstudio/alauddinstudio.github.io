A cozy little story

Grandpa Bramble, the old beekeeper, hands you his hives. Walk little Honey the bear cub around the farm, collect honeycomb from the bees and turn it into honey jars, candles and pretty gift boxes.

🐝Busy beesBees fly from the flowers and fill a jar by each hive, one comb at a time.
🕯️Craft sweet goodsBuild the Wax Press, Candle Workshop and Gift Shop. Every step is worth more.
🏡Hut to manorFive levels, from a Flower Meadow to the golden Honey Manor.
👑Rare beesBumble, Golden, Rose, Rainbow and the Queen Bee, each faster than the last.
