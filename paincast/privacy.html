Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Paincast ("the app") is made by Md Alauddin (Alauddin Studio). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: your diary never leaves your phone, there is no account, no ads and no tracking across other apps.

Your pain diary

Everything you log (how you feel, what hurts, medicine names, notes) is stored only on your phone. We never receive it. If you use "Export diary", the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate (city-level) location once to find your city. You can also type a city instead. To get the forecast, the app sends rounded coordinates (about 1 km) to our server, which asks MET Norway for the forecast and keeps a short-lived copy so nearby users share it. The server does not store who asked or keep a log of locations. City names come from your phone's own map service (Apple or Google).

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether Premium is active using an anonymous ID. We never see your card details.

Notifications

Alerts are planned on your phone from the forecast. No push server is involved.

Crash reports and app usage

To find bugs and improve the app, we use Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics and Remote Config). Google processes this data for us as a service provider.

  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, it sends technical details: the error, the app version, the phone model and system version.
  • App usage: simple events, such as which screens are opened, whether the Premium page was shown and whether a purchase was made.
  • These come with a random app ID made by Firebase and an approximate region (country or city) that Google works out from your IP address. They are not linked to your name, email or advertising ID.
  • The app does not collect the advertising ID and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your pain diary, symptoms, medicines and notes are never sent.
  • Remote Config lets us change simple settings, such as the text on the Premium page, without a new app version.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No ads and no advertising ID
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No health data on our server

Not medical advice

Paincast shows weather forecasts and your own notes. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can delete all app data at any time in Settings → Delete all my data, or by deleting the app. For questions or requests, including GDPR and CCPA requests, email hello@alauddinstudio.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
