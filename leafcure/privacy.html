Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Leafcure ("the app") is made by Md Alauddin (Alauddin Studio). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your photos are not stored on our server.

Photos you scan

  • When you scan a plant, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to create the diagnosis.
  • We do not store your photos on our server. They are used only to create the diagnosis.
  • Anthropic processes the photos as our service provider under its commercial terms. It does not use them to train its AI models and deletes them after a limited time.
  • A copy of the photo and the diagnosis is saved on your device so you can see your History and My Plants. Deleting a scan removes it from your device.

Device identifier

  • The app creates a random ID on your device. It is sent with each scan only to limit how many scans one device can make per day, to prevent abuse.
  • It is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID. The daily counter is deleted after 48 hours.

Purchases

  • Subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • No account or login is needed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app. For questions or requests, including GDPR and CCPA requests, email alauddinahmed7888@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
