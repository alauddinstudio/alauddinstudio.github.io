Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Alif Garden ("the app") is a learning game for children aged 4 to 8, made by Md Alauddin (Alauddin Studio). This policy is written for parents. In short: the app collects no data at all. There is no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no links out of the app for children.

What stays on the phone

Your child's first name (or nickname), the letters learned, stars and garden are stored only on the phone, so the game remembers them. We never receive them. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them, under your control.

Sounds

Letter names are spoken by the phone's own text-to-speech voice, on the phone. Surah recitations are files inside the app.

Purchases

Purchases are made by a parent, after answering a grown-up question, and are handled by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether Premium is active using an anonymous ID. No personal information about your child is involved. We never see your card details.

Notifications

A weekly report for parents is planned on the phone. No push server is involved.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No ads and no advertising ID
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking
  • No links, purchases or settings without a grown-up question
  • No selling or sharing of any data

COPPA and GDPR-K

Because the app does not collect personal information from children, no parental consent for data collection is needed. If you believe we hold data about your child, email us and we will help.

Your rights

Delete all data at any time in Grown-ups → Delete all data, or by deleting the app. Questions: hello@alauddinstudio.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
