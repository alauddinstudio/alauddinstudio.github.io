Help

Questions and answers

Is it safe for my child?

Yes. No ads, no links out, no account and no data collected. Purchases and settings are behind a grown-up question.

What is free and what is Premium?

Free: the first 5 letters, Al-Fatiha and Al-Ikhlas, part of the garden. Premium: all 28 letters, short vowels, 10 short surahs, the full garden, 3 children and the weekly report, with a 7-day free trial.

Where do the sounds come from?

Letter names use your phone's own Arabic voice. Surahs are recited by Aaqib Azeez (public domain). The Quran text is from Tanzil.net, used exactly as published.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Alif Garden → Cancel Subscription.
Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Alif Garden → Cancel.
Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

I bought Premium but the app still asks me to pay.

Open Settings and tap Restore, using the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If that does not help, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or the Google Play Help Center.

How do I delete my data?

Open Grown-ups → Delete all data, or delete the app. Everything is only on your phone.
